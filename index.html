Voice commands

When anything you say contains one of the phrases, he runs that bit. "Say exactly" is instant and works with no brain. "Improvise" hands the instruction to his brain. Put [squawk] or [whistle] anywhere to play the sound.

His voice

These are the voices built into your phone. Higher pitch sounds more like a bird.

Bird sounds

Getting the sound into the bird

  1. Pair the bird's Bluetooth board in your phone's Bluetooth settings, the same way you do for music.
  2. Turn the phone's volume most of the way up. The beak moves with loudness, so quiet audio means a lazy beak.
  3. Tap Test voice. If you hear it from the phone instead of the bird, reconnect the board and try again.

His brain

Hands-free listening

Inside the Claude app the microphone is switched off, so use the text box and your keyboard's mic. For tap-to-talk listening, use the hosted copy:

  1. Open lisamskinner.github.io/squawker in Safari and allow the microphone.
  2. Paste your API key above. It's saved only in Safari on this phone.
  3. Keep it in Safari. On iPhone a home-screen icon can't use the mic. For a one-tap launcher, make a Siri shortcut with the Open URLs action.
  4. If the drum never hears you, turn on Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard.
