# Squawker's Perch

A phone app for talking with a Squawker McCaw animatronic parrot. You talk, he answers in his own personality, and the phone speaks the reply over Bluetooth so his beak moves.

**Open it:** https://lisamskinner.github.io/squawker/

## How it works

- **Listening:** tap the drum and talk. On iPhone this needs Safari itself (not a home-screen icon) with Dictation turned on.
- **Brain:** Claude, using your own Anthropic API key. Paste it in the Brain tab. The key is saved only in that browser on your phone and is never in this code.
- **Voice:** the phone's built-in voices at a high pitch, plus squawk and whistle sounds.
- **Personality:** a mix of the Tiki Room showbird, a Jungle Cruise skipper and the Beak and Barrel's Rummy, plus a backstory, catchphrases, house rules and voice commands, all editable in the app.

Everything is one file: `index.html`.
